# Contributing

This is a living toolkit for the people who take developer tools and AI products to market: founders, early GTM hires, and founding AEs. Contributions that make it sharper are welcome.

## What makes a good contribution

- **Real thresholds, not principles.** "Charge more" is a principle. "If your top-decile users would pay 3× and churn <5%, you're underpriced" is a threshold.
- **The mistake included.** The most useful frameworks name the thing that *looks* reasonable but quietly fails.
- **A specific example.** Anonymous is fine, vague is not. Numbers earn trust.
- **Founder's language.** Direct, concrete, no marketing fluff. If a developer would roll their eyes, rewrite it.

## How

1. Open an **issue** describing the gap or the framework you'd add (which skill, what's missing).
2. Or open a **PR**:
   - New skill → `skills/<kebab-name>/SKILL.md` with the standard frontmatter (`name`, `description`) and the standard sections (when to use · framework · decision tree · mistakes · example · 30-minute checklist).
   - Editing a skill → keep the voice and structure consistent.
3. Cite your source if a framework comes from published work (we credit **Frankl** and **Czakon** throughout; add others as needed).

## Not a fit

- Generic "growth hacking" tactics, engagement-bait, or anything that tricks developers. The first rule of dev GTM is: don't lie to developers. They find out.

Thanks for helping founders, GTM hires, and founding AEs get out of their own way.
